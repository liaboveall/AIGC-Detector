# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless
of background, identity, experience level, or viewpoint. Technical disagreement is
welcome; personal attacks, discrimination, intimidation, and unwanted disclosure of
private information are not.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific, constructive, and evidence-based.
- Respect dataset restrictions, privacy, and the limits of model-generated scores.
- Give credit for ideas, code, experiments, and reports.
- Avoid overstating results or presenting development evidence as an official score.
- Keep private images, credentials, and restricted data out of public discussions.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Encouraging unsafe or deceptive use of the detector.
- Knowingly fabricating benchmarks, provenance, or contribution claims.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub. Reports
will be reviewed in good faith, kept as confidential as practical, and handled with a
response proportionate to the behavior. Maintainers may edit or remove content and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.
